Explore the texts

The atlas maps places mentioned in texts from 1450–1700 collected through Renascor.

  • Search or select a place to see the documents that mention it.
  • Choose a period with the date selectors or by dragging across the timeline.
  • Read the points: larger means more mentions; blue means earlier document dates, red means later.
  • Switch to Letters to follow correspondence from where it was sent to where it was received.

Understand the sources

Under Filters & display, choose:

  • Editor sources: places identified in scholarly editions and located using the editions’ own information.
  • Expanded sources: also includes places located by name matching or identified automatically in texts. These additions may contain errors.

Counts reflect the texts included: large collections weigh more heavily. Countries and regions appear as single points, too.
